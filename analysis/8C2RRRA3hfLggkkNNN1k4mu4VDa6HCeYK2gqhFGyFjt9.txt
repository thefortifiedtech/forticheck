✦ Analysis of the token Hormcrs (8C2RRR...) reveals that while it appears "organic," it is actually a highly efficient, small-scale liquidity-capture scam. The detection logic for these tokens is different from "hard rugs" because the
  goal is to trap a small number of retail buyers rather than a massive crowd.

  Here is the breakdown of the pattern and the steps to implement in your bot.

  ---

  1. The Pattern: "The Nano-Pump"
   * Target: These bots target tokens with extremely low initial liquidity (e.g., ~$2k).
   * The Trick: The developer launches the token and immediately transfers 99%+ of the supply to a "Vault" or secondary wallet (FhVo3m...HLuM).
   * The Pump: A few "rat trader" or "fresh" wallets buy small amounts to create "green candles" on 1m charts. 
   * The Exit: As soon as retail buys ($50–$100), the developer dumps from the secondary wallet. Because liquidity is so thin, even a $50 buy causes a massive price spike, and a $50 dump wipes it out.

  ---

  2. Bot Detection Steps (The "Organic" Audit)

  To detect these tokens before they dump, your bot should execute these logic gates in order:

  Step A: Supply Hijack Check (High Priority)
   * Trigger: New token launch on Solana (Raydium/Meteora).
   * Logic: Query top holders. If any single wallet (other than the LP/DEX address) holds >50% of the supply, flag it immediately.
   * Refinement: Look for transfer_in tags. If a wallet received 90% of the supply via transfer instead of buying, it's a "dump wallet."
   * In this token: TOP1 held 99.5% via transfer.

  Step B: The "Rat" Cluster Test
   * Logic: Check for multiple "Fresh Wallets" or "Rat Traders" buying within the first 60 seconds of launch. 
   * Filter: If ≥3 wallets with the same funding source or "fresh" status buy simultaneously, it’s a coordinated pump.
   * In this token: Multiple fresh wallets bought at the exact same $0.0004 price point.

  Step C: Liquidity-to-Supply Skew
   * Logic: Compare liquidity to market_cap. 
   * Ratio: If Liquidity / Market Cap < 0.1 (i.e., Market Cap is 10x larger than Liquidity), the price is extremely fragile. 
   * Signal: An organic token usually has deeper liquidity relative to its initial cap to allow for growth. A "pump and dump" keeps liquidity thin to maximize price impact for the scammer.

  Step D: Dev Activity History
   * Logic: Query dev.twitter_create_token_count and dev.creator_open_count.
   * Red Flag: If the developer has launched multiple tokens (>3) in a short period (days) with creator_close status on all of them, it’s a serial pumper.
